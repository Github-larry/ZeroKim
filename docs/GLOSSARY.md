# Glossary (plain English)

- **Break-even win rate**: the share of trades you must win just to end even, given the payout. Below it you lose over time.
- **Margin**: the built-in price gap between two opposite contracts; the house's cut.
- **Sample size**: how many observations a statistic is based on. Small samples mislead.
- **Confidence interval**: a range likely to contain the true value; wide means unsure.
- **Multiple-testing correction**: when you test many things, some look special by luck; this adjusts for that.
- **Martingale**: doubling the stake after a loss. Forbidden in Zerotrader.
  More terms are added as features are built.
